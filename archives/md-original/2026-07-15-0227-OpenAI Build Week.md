Project Description: English
BuilderOS is an AI-assisted visual website production workspace built for teams.
Website projects are often scattered across design tools, chat threads, content management systems, review documents, and deployment services. BuilderOS brings planning, design, content management, team review, and publishing into one place. It helps designers, marketers, and business teams move from an idea to a working website with fewer handoffs and less rework.
The process begins with a guided conversation. Instead of requiring users to write a perfect prompt, BuilderOS asks simple questions about the business, target audience, goals, and visual direction. It then creates three distinct and fully editable design proposals.
Users can ask AI to update a single layer, redesign a section, revise a page, or make coordinated changes across an entire website. Every AI edit is reviewable and non-destructive. Users can compare alternatives, inspect the changes, approve or reject them, and undo an applied edit.
BuilderOS also includes a responsive visual editor, reusable components and variants, CMS collections and dynamic pages, image and Open Graph generation, SEO and accessibility audits, comments, version history, real-time collaboration, staging and production publishing, and Static HTML or React code export.
Codex with GPT-5.6 helped build and validate the core product, including the visual editor, AI workflows, CMS, collaboration features, publishing system, and automated tests. It allowed a large product concept to be developed step by step while keeping the architecture, implementation, and user experience consistent.
BuilderOS belongs in the Work & Productivity category because it turns website production from a fragmented chain of tasks into one clear, reviewable workflow. Teams can create, improve, review, and publish websites faster while keeping full control over the final result.
